## Presentation Guidelines

### Design
- Use institution template if available
- Professional, clean design
- Consistent color scheme (cyber-themed)
- Readable fonts (minimum 18pt)
- High-quality images and screenshots

### Content
- Clear, concise bullet points
- Avoid text-heavy slides
- Use diagrams and visualizations
- Include code snippets sparingly
- Demonstrate with screenshots

### Timing
- Proposal: 10-15 minutes
- Mid-term: 15-20 minutes
- Final Review: 25-30 minutes
- Defense: 30-40 minutes

### Delivery Tips
- Practice presentation multiple times
- Time yourself
- Prepare for common questions
- Have backup demo plan
- Bring notes but don't read from slides

## Common Questions to Prepare

1. **Technical**
   - Why did you choose Docker over VMs?
   - How do you handle container security?
   - What happens if multiple users access the same challenge?
   - How scalable is your solution?

2. **Design**
   - Why JWT instead of session-based authentication?
   - How did you design the database schema?
   - What's your approach to state management?

3. **Comparison**
   - How is CyTutor different from Hack The Box?
   - What advantages does your platform offer?
   - What are the limitations?

4. **Future**
   - How will you maintain challenge content?
   - Plans for production deployment?
   - Potential for commercialization?
